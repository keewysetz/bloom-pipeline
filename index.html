03Where deals leak

What is holding, what is slipping, and where the terms do not match.

What's working

  • The casual DM opener, and the step into a group chat with the team, gets real conversations going.
  • Charlie is strong on calls. The vision, the demo, and stakeholders not mercenaries land.
  • Custom deals show flexibility. Galileo and oatmilk are the examples.

What's hurting

  • Numbers drift from call to call. Retainer, stake, reward pool, referral share, cohort size, and competitions have all been described differently.
  • No clear close at the end of a call. Ines asked for a 10-day trial and got a vague answer. Gyo left with explore the platform.
  • Follow-up is slow. Bloom owes a deal structure or a breakdown, and it lands days later. Hustlepedia waited 22 days.
  • The deal is not matched to the trader before the call. Gyo's roughly $15M a week is mostly farming. His directional trades are $10K to $30K, and that only came out on the call.
  • Group chats go quiet. Dime and Scooter stalled, with no owner pushing a decision.
